================================================================================
 CORRECT BAND GAP ANALYSIS - SPIN-POLARIZED DFT+U
 Binary (Mn,X)WO₄ Tungstates
================================================================================

METHODOLOGY:
  - Spin-polarized calculations (nspin=2)
  - For each k-point in each spin channel:
      gap(k,spin) = CBM(k,spin) - VBM(k,spin)
  - Overall band gap = min(gap) across all k and spins
